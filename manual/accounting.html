Accounting rules

New organizations default to EUR, the Netherlands, Europe/Amsterdam, Dutch VAT codes, a compact RGS-aligned chart, and monthly fiscal periods. Finalized invoices and approved expenses create balanced journal entries. Posted entries are corrected through reversals; document versions and artifact hashes preserve the evidence used at finalization.

Reports are grouped on the Reports page:

GroupReports
VATVAT return, VAT by tax code, intra-EU supplies (ICP), EU distance sales (OSS), small businesses scheme
FinancialFinancial statements, profit and loss, balance sheet, cash flow, money in and out, unpaid invoices
InsightsCustomers, suppliers, products and services, cost categories, trips timeline
BookkeepingTrial balance, general ledger

Exports include CSV, XLSX, PDF, XAF 4.0, and UBL artifacts. XAF and UBL are exchange files: Fiscal Fox does not transmit Peppol messages or file returns directly with Dutch authorities.

VAT

The VAT page shows the year as four quarters, each with its status (not started, in progress, ready to file, deadline passed), the amount to pay or reclaim, and the filing deadline: the last day of the month after the quarter. Selecting a quarter opens its return laid out like the Belastingdienst form:

SectionBoxes
11a 21%, 1b 9%, 1c other rates, 1d private use, 1e 0% or not taxed
22a domestic reverse charge
33a exports outside the EU, 3b EU supplies, 3c installation and distance sales
44a and 4b purchases from outside and inside the EU
55a VAT due, 5b input tax, 5c to pay (negative means reclaim)

How the return is calculated:

  • It is accrual based on the booking date of posted journal entries, not on payment. Drafts are excluded; credit notes and refunds reduce the period, and reversals restore what they reverse.
  • Each tax code's box mapping decides where its turnover and VAT go. A box only takes lines from its own side of the return, so a code used on both sales and purchases (such as NL-ZERO) never puts purchases in sales box 1e.
  • All deductible input tax goes to 5b. 5a is the VAT of sections 1 to 4, and 5c = 5a − 5b.
  • Reverse-charged purchases carry no supplier VAT. When such an expense is approved, Fiscal Fox self-assesses the VAT (rate × net amount): it is due in the purchase box (4b for NL-REVERSE-PURCHASE) and deductible in 5b, so the net effect is zero when fully deductible. An expense line with a reverse-charge tax code and a non-zero VAT amount is rejected.
  • Amounts to file are whole euros rounded in the filer's favour, as the Belastingdienst allows: turnover and VAT due are rounded down and input tax is rounded up. 5a and 5c are summed from the rounded boxes. The exact amounts stay available in the report and exports.
  • Tax codes mapped to boxes that are not on the Dutch return still count towards the totals and produce a warning on the report.

VAT by tax code shows the same quarter per tax code, split into sales and purchases, with document counts. Its net amount reconciles with box 5c of the return for the same period.

Below the return, the VAT page checks the other Dutch declarations:

  • ICP (opgaaf intracommunautaire prestaties): finalized invoice lines with an intra-EU tax code, per customer VAT number, split into goods (catalog products) and services (catalog services and free-text lines). Its total should match box 3b. Customers without a VAT number or outside the other EU member states are flagged.
  • OSS: distance sales of goods (catalog products with a standard or zero-rated tax code) to consumers, meaning customers without a VAT number, in other EU countries, per country. The calendar-year total is compared with the €10,000 threshold for charging the customer's local VAT through the One Stop Shop; last year's total counts too. Services to consumers are normally taxed in the Netherlands and are left out, with a warning, because electronic, telecom, and broadcasting services do count and Fiscal Fox cannot tell them apart.
  • KOR: turnover in boxes 1a to 1e for the year against the €20,000 limit of the small businesses scheme. The limit also applies to the year before.

The customer's country is the invoice delivery country, falling back to the billing address. Corrections of a filed return up to €1,000 can go in the next return; larger ones need a suppletie. Each section links to the relevant Belastingdienst page (in Dutch: the English pages are written for businesses outside the Netherlands).

Financial statements and insights

Financial statements show profit and loss and the balance sheet for a calendar year next to the year before. While the year is still running, both years cover 1 January up to the same day, so they compare like with like. Total equity includes the result of earlier years and of the current year.

Cash flow shows the bank, cash, and Stripe clearing accounts month by month: opening balance, money in, money out, and closing balance.

Insights read finalized documents, posted expenses, time, and trips:

  • Customer insight: invoiced amount (excluding VAT), unpaid amount (including VAT), hours, and kilometres per customer.
  • Supplier insight: posted expenses per supplier.
  • Products and services: quantity, revenue, and average price per catalog item.
  • Cost categories: posted amounts per expense account and their share.
  • Trips timeline: trips in order, flagging trips that overlap another trip by the same person.

Drafts

  • Expenses without a receipt. Add without receipt on the Expenses page records an expense by hand (date, supplier, net amount, VAT, tax code, and expense account). It is saved as a draft and cannot be approved until a receipt is uploaded with Attach receipt. Attaching keeps the typed amounts and moves the expense to needs-review. The dashboard's to-do list counts the drafts that still need a receipt. One-line expenses under review can be corrected with Edit before approval.
  • Draft contacts. The customer picker on invoices and billable conversions, and the supplier picker on manual expenses, search as you type. When no contact has the typed name, the list offers + Add “name”, which saves a name-only draft customer or supplier and selects it. Draft contacts are marked in Contacts; Add details completes them. Finalizing a quote, invoice, or credit note for a draft contact is refused until its details are added. A saved contact can widen to both customer and supplier but cannot switch from one to the other.
  • Invoice and quote drafts can be edited, lines included, until they are finalized.

Contacts, catalog, and projects

  • A contact is a private person or a business. Businesses can record their Dutch legal form: eenmanszaak (ZZP), VOF, maatschap, CV, BV, NV, stichting, vereniging, coöperatie, or other.
  • The invoice email receives invoices and payment reminders. Left blank, the contact email is used. Send asks to confirm that address, or for one when the contact has none.
  • Catalog prices and project hourly rates have at most two decimals, and no more than the currency allows. Catalog items and projects can be edited at any time: invoices and quotes already created keep their own prices, and new ones use the new values. Every price or rate change is recorded in the audit trail and shown as the item's Price history.
  • A new invoice or quote line can start from a catalog item, which fills the description, unit, price, VAT, and revenue account.

Contract templates

A template is either HTML with {{variables}} or an uploaded PDF. A PDF is sent as is: when the contract is finalized, Fiscal Fox keeps the PDF's hash in the contract snapshot and appends its signature page after the last page; the signed copy adds the signature certificate there. Editing a template saves a new version. Drafts use the latest version when they are finalized, while finalized contracts keep the snapshot they were signed from.

Mileage

A new trip's rate per kilometre defaults to the Belastingdienst's maximum tax-free allowance for the year the trip started, and follows the start date until a vehicle or a typed rate replaces it:

YearRate per km
2022€0.19
2023€0.21
2024€0.23
2025€0.23
2026€0.25

Earlier years use €0.19, and later years use the newest rate until it is added. New vehicles default to the current year's rate. Rates for later years are added when the Belastingdienst announces them.

Dashboard

The dashboard is made of widgets: key numbers, quick actions, revenue and expenses for the last 12 months, money to receive and to pay, the last 30 days of invoices, costs, hours, and trips, the to-do list, recent activity, and the company profile. Widgets chooses which ones show; the choice is saved per organization in the browser, and Reset to default restores it.

Safeguards

Fiscal period locks and normal workflow controls protect posting integrity. Administrative force overrides, direct SQL, or privileged migration-role access can bypass application controls and row-level security. Treat any override as an incident-like operation: authorize it, back up first, record the reason, verify balanced journals and artifact hashes, and retain an external audit trail.

The application is not a substitute for professional accounting or tax advice. Validate mappings, VAT treatment, retention, and filing obligations for the specific organization.
