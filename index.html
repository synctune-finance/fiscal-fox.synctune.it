What it does

The admin around your business, in one place.

  • Sales

    Quotes, invoices, credit notes, recurring invoices, reminders and Peppol-ready UBL files.

  • Expenses

    Upload a receipt, check what was read from it, and approve it into the books.

  • Dutch VAT

    The quarterly btw-aangifte box by box, with ICP, OSS and KOR checks and filing deadlines.

  • Time and trips

    Log hours and business trips, approve them, and invoice the billable ones.

  • Reports

    Financial statements, cash flow, insights, trial balance and exports for your accountant.

  • Audit trail

    Every change is recorded, posted entries are corrected by reversal, and files keep their fingerprints.

Your infrastructure

Run it yourself.

Fiscal Fox runs with Docker Compose: a web app, an API, a background worker, PostgreSQL, Valkey and S3-compatible storage. Each organization's data is separated by PostgreSQL row-level security.

Receipts are read locally by default. Remote AI providers, Stripe, DocuSign, calendars and maps are optional, and you decide which ones to connect.

Clear boundaries

It prepares. You hand off.

Fiscal Fox does not connect to your bank, host your business email, transmit Peppol messages or file tax returns. It prepares the numbers and the XAF and UBL files you give to your accountant or access point.

It is software, not professional accounting or tax advice. Check VAT treatment, retention and filing obligations for your business.
